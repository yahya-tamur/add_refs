How to annotate

Shift+drag over the text of a definition to highlight it (repeat to add more lines, even on other pages).

Then drag a box around the symbol or phrase being defined and type it, e.g. simple equivalence or \Sigma_{ij}.

Drag over a later use of a term to underline it. Type the term; if it matches a definition, it becomes a link in that definition's colour.

Boxes and underlines over an English phrase fill it in for you; over a symbol, the app reads it from the page image and suggests LaTeX (e.g. \Sigma_{\Lambda}).

Scanned PDFs without a text layer are read with OCR in the background the first time they are opened (progress in the status bar).

Annotations autosave in this browser. Export PDF saves a copy with the marks and links that any viewer can show; open that copy here to keep editing.

Click a link to jump; Alt+← goes back. Right-click a mark to edit or delete it. Esc cancels, Ctrl+F searches, Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+wheel zooms.
